Collaborative AI planning.

Where all three meet

Coplanning

Authority, lived experience and machine intelligence working as one system: the institution holds the mandate and the guardrails, people bring local knowledge and what they value, and AI turns both into options that can be seen, tested and agreed on.

A plan that arrives already carrying its agreement, rather than going looking for approval afterwards.

02 What CoPlanAI is

The collaborative planning platform for cities — and the citizens reshaping their future.

In a workshop — on-site, online, in a classroom or a museum — participants transform real photos of a real place, then choose between AI-generated options. It runs in four forms of collaboration, and the output is always a mandate: documented, traceable, and defensible in a public meeting.

03 How it works

One platform, two interfaces: the planning studio and the public square.

For city design offices

The planning studio

Planning teams co-plan internally — configuring agents, curating sites and shaping the session, before anything goes public. Departments across the institution use the same studio for design charrettes and policy evaluations.

For residents & the public

The public square

Citizens and stakeholders take part in person or online — on mobile, laptop or iPad. On a tablet they can transform scenarios by drawing straight on the image.

Draw straight on the site — on a tablet or kiosk.
