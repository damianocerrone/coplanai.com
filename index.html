Coplanning: where three kinds of knowledge meet

Interactive diagram. Use Tab to move between the three roles, their overlaps and the centre; the description panel updates for each.

Where all three meet

Coplanning

Authority, lived experience and machine intelligence working as one system: the institution holds the mandate and the guardrails, people bring local knowledge and what they value, and AI turns both into options that can be seen, tested and agreed on.

A plan that arrives already carrying its agreement, rather than going looking for approval afterwards.

04 · the platform

The platform for planners and citizens to coplan the city

One engagement, two surfaces. Planners run it from the Studio; the street takes part from a phone with no account. The same record carries both sides — and it is the real product below, not a mock-up.

CoPlan Studio

The desktop workspace where a planning team turns a written brief into checked, rendered design options.

The Configurator
